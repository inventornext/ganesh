Patent In Progress · Manuscript in Revision

CF-ET — Constraint-Aware Transformer

A deep learning architecture for counterfactual grid decision support. The core idea is a Kronecker-product feasibility mask applied directly to transformer attention scores before softmax, so that recommended grid actions are guaranteed to respect physical operating constraints rather than being filtered after the fact.

Built as a full 12-file PyTorch codebase with a Flask API and an operator demo interface. Initial submission to IEEE Transactions on Artificial Intelligence was desk-rejected; the work is being strengthened with additional experiments, proofs, and baselines for resubmission to IEEE Transactions on Smart Grid or IEEE TNNLS.

Patent application in progress via IIT Kharagpur's Technology Transfer Cell.

Targeting IEEE TSG

PAE — Privacy-Preserving Smart Grid Architecture

An architecture for protecting household-level privacy in smart meter data collection, using Chaum blind-signature tokens and data-concentrator-level aggregation. Grounded in India's CEA smart meter specifications and the RDSS smart-metering rollout, and validated on the IEEE 33-bus feeder.

Currently weighing target journal — IEEE Transactions on Smart Grid vs. an editorially-connected alternative.

Under Review · Journal of Energy Storage

MES — Mobile Energy Storage Dispatch

A dispatch strategy for mobile energy storage units, addressing how to schedule and route distributed, mobile storage assets across a grid to balance load and support reliability.

Manuscript EST-D-26-05875, submitted April 2026, currently under review.
