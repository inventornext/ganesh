ABOUT

Ganesh Kumar Pandey

PhD Researcher, Department of Electrical Engineering, IIT Kharagpur.

Background

I'm a PhD candidate in Electrical Engineering at IIT Kharagpur, working under the supervision of Prof. Debapriya Das. My thesis has been submitted and my defense is currently pending. My research sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence, mathematical optimization, and power systems — with a particular focus on smart grids and AI-driven grid decision support.

Rather than building isolated algorithms, I try to design technologies capable of solving real infrastructure problems: keeping grid operator recommendations physically feasible, protecting consumer privacy in smart metering, and making distributed energy storage easier to dispatch efficiently.

I have two published journal papers, with additional work currently under review and in preparation. Alongside my research, I'm working with IIT Kharagpur's Technology Transfer Cell on a patent application for a novel AI architecture developed during my PhD.

What I'm Working Toward

My long-term goal is a faculty position at an IIT, continuing research on AI-driven power systems and mentoring the next generation of engineers working on grid modernization.
