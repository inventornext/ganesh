ABOUT

Ganesh Kumar Pandey

PhD Researcher, Department of Electrical Engineering, IIT Kharagpur.

Background

I'm a PhD candidate in Electrical Engineering at IIT Kharagpur, working under the supervision of Prof. Debapriya Das . My synopsis seminar and thesis submission are scheduled for August 2026. My research develops optimization algorithms and decentralized market architectures for renewable-integrated power grids, a peer-to-peer blockchain energy market with on-chain settlement, a new metaheuristic optimizer, and closed-form dispatch models for mobile storage, each validated on IEEE benchmark networks and carried through to journal publication.

I'm comfortable moving between mathematical formulation, algorithm design, and deployable Python/MATLAB/Solidity code. I also have work in progress on AI for grid decision support and privacy-preserving smart grid architecture, along with a patent application in progress, details on both will be shared once further along.

I have one published journal paper, with three more in journal revision or under review.
